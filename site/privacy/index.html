Privacy, plainly.

Cafade is designed to work locally. This policy describes what the app and this website handle.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

Cafade is published by ONE SHOT STAR. Questions about this policy can be sent to i+cafade@oneshotstar.com.

What Cafade stores

Cafade does not require an account. Drink entries, custom caffeine values, settings, and manual bedtime preferences are stored on your device for the app to function.

Cafade does not sell your entries, use them for advertising, or send your personal caffeine log to a server operated by ONE SHOT STAR.

HealthKit

HealthKit is optional. When you enable it, Cafade can write the entries you choose as Dietary Caffeine. The first release does not read sleep data or other HealthKit data.

HealthKit data is handled through Apple’s HealthKit framework and remains subject to Apple’s policies. Cafade does not use HealthKit data for advertising or sell it to data brokers.

You can deny HealthKit permission and continue using Cafade’s in-app log. You can also pause new Health writes inside Cafade without deleting samples already saved in Apple Health.

Share cards and Photos

Cafade creates share-card images on your device from the entries already stored in the app. The image leaves the app only when you choose Share or Save Image.

Save Image requests add-only Photos access. Cafade does not read your photo library.

Subscriptions and RevenueCat

Cafade uses Apple’s App Store for payment. RevenueCat helps Cafade load products, complete purchases, restore purchases, and determine whether the Pro entitlement is active.

RevenueCat may process an anonymous App User ID, device and app information needed for the SDK, purchase history, entitlement status, and Apple transaction or receipt information. Cafade does not send your drink entries or HealthKit data to RevenueCat.

RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy and Apple’s terms apply to their respective services.

This website

This website is a static site. It does not use advertising cookies, analytics scripts, account forms, or third-party tracking pixels.

If you email support, your email provider and the contents of your message are handled according to their policies. We use the information you send only to respond to your request and keep a support record when needed.

Deletion and changes

Delete individual entries in the app from History, or use Delete all data in Settings. If Cafade cannot immediately remove a Health sample that it previously wrote, it stores the sample identifier locally so you can retry cleanup from Settings.

Because the app stores its log locally, deleting the app also removes its local app data unless your device restores it from a backup. Removing the app does not automatically remove data already written to Apple Health.

We may update this policy when the app changes. The date above identifies the latest version.

Contact

For privacy questions, email i+cafade@oneshotstar.com.
